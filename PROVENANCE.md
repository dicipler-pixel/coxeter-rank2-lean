## Public record of the dihedral isomorphism

The dihedral isomorphism in `DihedralBridge.lean` was first made public by Jeromie Beasley as
Mathlib pull request
[leanprover-community/mathlib4#43703](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/43703),
"feat(GroupTheory/Coxeter): identify rank-two Coxeter groups with dihedral groups", opened on
11 September 2026 and closed unmerged on 7 October 2026. Its commits and review history remain
public there. This repository is the maintained home of the result; it is not a Mathlib
submission.
